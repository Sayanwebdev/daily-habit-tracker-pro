1. Introduction

Welcome to Habit Tracker Pro ("we," "us," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly:

  • Habit Information: Names, descriptions, categories, and frequency of habits you track
  • Check-in Data: Daily records of completed habits, dates, and personal notes
  • Account Information: Email address (if you opt to use contact features)

2.2 Data Collected Automatically:

  • Browser Data: Browser type, device type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, interaction patterns with features
  • Local Storage: Your habits and check-in data stored locally on your device
  • Cookies: Small files stored to enhance user experience and remember preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
  • To personalize your experience and track your progress
  • To generate analytics and statistics about your habits
  • To send important updates or changes to our policies
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

Local Storage: Most of your habit data is stored locally in your browser using browser storage mechanisms (localStorage). This means your data primarily remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly choose to export or backup your data.

Data Security: We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention: Your data is retained locally until you manually delete it. We do not retain copies of exported data on our servers unless explicitly stored by you.

5. Third-Party Services

Habit Tracker Pro may use third-party services for:

  • Chart.js: For displaying statistics and visualizations
  • Tailwind CSS: For styling and user interface design
  • Analytics (if applicable): To understand how users interact with our App

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies for more information about their data collection practices.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access and view your data anytime within the App
  • Download/Export: You can export your data in JSON format for backup or portability
  • Delete: You can delete individual habits or clear all data at any time
  • Opt-Out: You can choose not to provide certain information, though this may limit functionality
