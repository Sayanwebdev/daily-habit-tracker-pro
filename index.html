Goal Tracking Explained

You can track habits in two ways:

  • Binary: Simple checkbox (Done/Not Done). Counts as 100% completion if checked.
  • Quantifiable: Set a numerical goal (e.g., 8 km). When you enter **2 km**, the habit shows 25% progress.

Calendar & Progress Indicators

Each day on the calendar shows two numbers:

  • Fraction (e.g., 1/2): The number of habits **fully completed (100% reached)** over the total number of habits. This is used for streak calculation.
  • Percentage (e.g., 50%): The overall weighted percentage of all habits, factoring in partial completion for Quantifiable goals.

Color Guide:

  • 100% Overall Progress (Great Job!)
  • 75% - 99% Progress
  • 50% - 74% Progress
  • 1% - 49% Progress
